Feast of Fusion

Where Belgian Sweetness Meets Sankarankovil

At WAFFLE ORBIT, we believed foodies in Sankarankovil deserved the same artisanal dessert quality found in metro cities. We imported heavy cast-iron waffle presses, developed our signature feather-light batter, and introduced the groundbreaking zero-leak sealed UFO Burger to the town!
